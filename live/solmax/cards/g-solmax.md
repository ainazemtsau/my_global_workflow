## goal
Solmax coherently supports both a first product/application branch,
Zaratusta, and a reusable operating-substrate architecture branch for
AI-led stateful owner/project processes.

## done_when
All hold unless the charter is explicitly revised:

1. Zaratusta reaches its branch success criteria: the owner relies on it
   deeply and recurringly in at least 3 distinct areas of life/work;
   live capabilities span tool, channel, memory and process axes without
   repeated core redesign; and at least one hard process is judged by the
   owner clearly better than a plain ChatGPT/Claude session.

2. The operating-substrate branch has owner-approved architecture/spec
   cartography that makes its question graph, dependencies, variants,
   research needs and proof anchors clear before design or
   implementation.

3. Zaratusta and operating-substrate coexist without treating current
   Zaratusta artifacts as substrate authority or placing substrate as a
   Zaratusta product child.

## why
Owner approved umbrella placement: Zaratusta remains the first
consumer/failure-case, while reusable operating-substrate is a separate
sibling architecture route.
