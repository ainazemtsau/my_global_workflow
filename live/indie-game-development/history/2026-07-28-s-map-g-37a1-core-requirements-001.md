**FOUR THINGS HE DECIDED THAT CHANGE THE GAME, not just the text.**

1. **Vertical is in, and the way up is the player's own work.** He rejected this leg's
   proposal of a flat sideways route with in-step gradients and replaced it with his own:
   the value is DOWN, the crew digs down and sideways, and there are no ready ladders or
   lifts — «игрок сам решает, да, как он будет забираться. Ну, прыжок, значит, у нас надо

   such: it makes the way out a consequence of how you dug, so «выкопал колодец и не
   оставил себе, как вылезти» becomes a legitimate death that is entirely the player's own
   authorship — the property he has wanted since the breach-panel rejection — at zero cost,
   because jumping and digging are already in the set. It also restores the strongest
   recorded image: a light kind rising into the shaft above you is rising into the very
   steps you cut to get out.

2. **PASSABILITY IS DELETED, and the card is better for it.** His answer to the row the
   verification could not read: «в карман можно войти в любой … если газ полностью выйдет,
   то карман — это обычное пространство тоже, уже выглубленное в породе … если газ вышел,
   то он спокойно заходит вообще без уронов. Вот этого физического пока делать не надо.»
   So the two kinds differ by the PRICE IN AIR and by nothing else; no physical barrier
   exists; and an emptied pocket is ordinary dug space that costs nothing to enter. This
   replaces criterion 5 in full. Recorded as an improvement and not merely as an answer:
   it turns a pocket from a pure downside into a LOOP — risk it, empty it, and you own a
   room you never had to cut — which is the first thing in the record that answers his own
   written doubt that the accepted reason is «слишком скучная, нединамичная … один трейд
   повторяется».

3. **NO SINKS, and this is the clock the concept was missing.** Asked where the substance
   goes when a pocket empties, he answered «никуда не должен деваться, да, он в шахте.» So
   emptying a pocket is RELOCATING it into your own workings, every breach makes the way
   back more expensive, and the direction's oldest unfixed problem — that waiting is always
   optimal (`work/converge-g-37a1.md` §11.2) — closes without a new mechanic.

4. **TWO GASES NOW; LIQUID IS HEADROOM, possibly as a REPLACEMENT.** He chose option C on
   the gas-versus-liquid fork and added a clause nobody had offered: liquid may later come
   in «как замену газа», not only as an addition. `i-substance-states-unpriced` is answered
   for the first core and stays open as a later fork.
