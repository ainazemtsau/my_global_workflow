## 1. One authority surface, no second plan

`TREE.md` owns the release outcome and R0–R5. Fresh `NOW.md`, exact CALL/RESULT
receipts and named product/player/Steam evidence own current truth. This policy
owns only invariant decision rules. It is not a backlog, scheduler, daily plan,
status snapshot or technical design. Demo Control Room and its dated appendices
are historical evidence only and have no current authority; its short tombstone
is a separate installation change.

Every control cycle is a disposable projection of exact fresh identities. It is
not copied forward as living truth. No controller artifact may introduce a
second task hierarchy, causal DAG, percent-complete model or technical HOW.

## 2. Authority and definition provenance

- The owner selects the release route, approves the Demo Basis and its meaning,
  approves every material new cut, chooses which 0..N outcomes to launch, and
  alone may activate February.
- Canon maintains and version-identifies the exact owner-approved Demo Basis.
- Demo Release Control consumes that identity, identifies evidence gaps,
  classifies forecast, recommends modes/cuts and ranks lawful outcomes. It may
  not redefine the basis, approve a cut, mutate a foreign root, choose technical
  HOW or claim a target result.
- Target tracks own technical HOW and return their own evidence. Direction
  sessions admit, close and route that evidence under the registered CALLs.

Definition-dependent CALLs carry literal
`demo_basis: <artifact>@<identity>` and require the same identity in returned
evidence. Independent work carries `basis_independent: <bounded scope>`.
Missing or stale identity fails closed. After B1 becomes B2, B1 consumers are
excluded and routed to target-owned reconciliation; old executor CALLs never
receive an updated product paste from the controller. A proof horizon is a
separate learning identity and never becomes accepted basis or DONE evidence.

## 4. Evidence contract

Each control claim names: `demo_basis`, `milestone@identity`, `claim_key`,
`predicted_observation`, `invalid_test_conditions`, `invalidates_if_failed`,
`resource_claims`, `reserved_exit`, `recompute_trigger` and, when applicable,
`basis_independent`.

Machine, owner, fresh-player and Steam evidence are distinct and do not replace
one another. Product commits, screenshots, owner LOOK, publication or a target
RESULT are inputs, not Direction close. A DONE claim needs evidence matching
the milestone plus a separate fresh binding G5/review. Public claims never
exceed representative evidence. R1 requires causal two-player expedition
evidence; R2 separately requires unfamiliar-player comprehension.

## 5. Forecast contract

Exactly one class is emitted, in this precedence:

1. `MISSED` — a selected-route hard gate passed unmet or eligibility is
   irreversibly false.
2. `UNFORECASTABLE` — any input required for a dated critical-path interval is
   missing. Render this as `NO DATE BASIS`, never as a percentage.
3. `CREDIBLE` — conservative upper bound plus an owner-set margin clears every
   remaining hard gate and no critical blocker exists.
4. `AT RISK` — inputs exist, but the conservative bound does not clear a gate.

The output names assumptions, interval, missing inputs, margin receipt and next
recompute trigger. Missing data is not zero; agent count is not throughput; an
October failure never activates February automatically.

## 6. Scoped operating modes

Modes restrict only the named affected surface and expire on their named receipt
or recompute trigger. They never freeze independent work by track label.

- `WAIT`: required authority, dependency or evidence is absent.
- `RECONCILE`: a basis or source identity changed and target-owned disposition
  is required before a replacement CALL.
- `STABILIZE`: an accepted proof surface is unstable; restore its already
  accepted invariant before expansion.
- `PROTECT`: a named shared resource or external gate is occupied, unavailable
  or UNKNOWN; avoid collision on that surface.
- `REPLAN`: evidence invalidated route, horizon or critical-path assumptions;
  recompute before definition-dependent launch.
- `PROBATION`: after a failure proves one missing control mechanism, test only
  that bounded mechanism. It is not permission to rebuild a living plan.

## 7. Cuts and recovery

The proof floor is protected: exact accepted basis, honest public claims, one
causal two-player expedition, fresh-player comprehension, reproducible
candidate/install/distribution evidence and external eligibility. The standing
cut order is optional Press Preview, SHOULD polish/variation, breadth of maps,
progression/economy, wide gas/tool/reaction sets, full destruction, eight-player,
dedicated-server and broad failure-matrix scope. A cut must name trigger, owner
approval receipt, evidence preserved and what becomes obsolete.

On blocker, failed proof, discard or material return: preserve evidence and
running custody; invalidate only the affected claim; re-read fresh identities;
select the scoped mode; then admit again. No automatic restart, route switch,
foreign mutation or add-back follows from recovery.

## 8. Lawful 0..N admission and controller rights

At start-day, a material return, owner verdict or hard-date checkpoint, a fresh
controller cycle may rank bounded observable outcomes. A candidate is excluded
only by current WIP/owner rule, missing authority/dependency, or a named shared
resource that is occupied, unavailable or UNKNOWN. Shared resources include
owner attention, worktree/file/Unity surfaces, physical/network machines,
review, integration, Deliver, Direction close and Valve queue.

Zero is lawful and creates no busywork. Several collision-free outcomes may be
shown together. The owner chooses 0..N; activation occurs in separate target
legs. The controller may issue only lawful bounded outcome requests and its own
same-track continuation. It never launches, pauses, retargets or closes a
foreign root. Character, Program or any other named track is not a global gate.

The disposable owner screen is at most 400 words and shows only: selected route
and next hard gate; latest real product/player result or `NONE`; exact accepted
basis and proof horizon; running plus ranked launchable 0..N; exclusions with
collision surfaces; protect/cut-next/cut-trigger; forecast class; and the next
recompute trigger. The ordinary outcome_dispatch root is READY on entry and
exit unless exact launch bookkeeping says otherwise.
