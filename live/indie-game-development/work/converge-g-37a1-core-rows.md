### A. The man and the launch

**ROW 1 — the game starts as a game.** (line 1 · criterion 1) `BUILD`

- **OBSERVABLE.** A named file exists. Double-clicking it on a machine that has never had Unity
  installed opens the game. Closing it and double-clicking again opens it again, in the same
  starting condition. No Editor, no console, no manual repair of any file.
- **OUTSIDER.** Hand the artifact to a person who has never opened Unity, say nothing but "start
  it, then start it again". PASS if both starts reach a state where a character responds to input.
  FAIL if either start needs a terminal, an editor, a file edit or a developer.
- **EVIDENCE.** The packaged player, run on a second physical machine with no Unity.
- **FORCES.** Which scenes the player contains; where the parameter home lives relative to the
  executable (couples to row 5). **"The same starting condition" is ANSWERED 2026-07-28:** a
  relaunch starts a fresh section, because nothing persists across a run at all (row 4). The
  row's second-launch clause is therefore checkable as written and no longer waits on anything.
- **HIGHEST LATE-DISCOVERY RISK IN THE SET.** The first clause of the card is the one with no
  price and no plan: `EditorBuildSettings` holds exactly one enabled scene (the empty URP template),
  nine real scenes exist and none is in the list, there is zero build automation anywhere in the
  product and zero player-build mention across 53 ADRs, and a gate receipt records the empty default
  as a deliberately preserved invariant. This needs no owner decision — criterion 1 already IS his

  Receipt: `history/2026-07-27-s-converge-verify-g-37a1-digging-card-001.md` V6.

**ROW 2 — the body.** (line 2 · criterion 3) `BUILD`

- **OBSERVABLE.** A controlled character walks on ground, jumps, leaves the ground and comes back
  down under gravity, stands on a surface, and cannot pass through rock or fall through the floor.
- **OUTSIDER.** Give the controls to someone who did not build it, instruction "walk, jump". PASS
  if they cross flat ground, jump onto a step and stand on it, jump into a hole and land at its
  bottom, and cannot walk through a wall. FAIL on any of: sliding through geometry, floating, no
  fall, a jump that never lands.
- **EVIDENCE.** The built player of row 1.
- **FORCES.** Walk speed; jump height; the height of a step the body mounts without jumping; fall
  behaviour; and the one that couples the body to everything else — **the body's size relative to
  the smallest diggable unit**, because it decides whether a single cut makes a hole a man fits in.
- **THIS ROW IS NEW WORK, NOT A CHECK.** The body in the product today integrates a strictly planar
  walk with Y written once at seed time and never again; there is no gravity, no ground contact and
  no collider read by movement code. Receipt: same, V7.

**ROW 3 — he gets back up himself.** (line 3 · criterion 3) `BUILD` `SETTING K5`

- **OBSERVABLE.** From the bottom of a shaft he cut himself, a route to the surface can be made
  using only the dig verb and the jump. The game contains no ladder, rope, lift, grapple, teleport
  or return-to-surface shortcut that substitutes for it. A player who cuts a shaft he cannot mount
  and does not cut steps is stuck, and being stuck resolves as a death (row 4), never as a freeze.
- **OUTSIDER.** (a) Ask an outsider to dig down at least three of their own jump-heights and get
  back. PASS if they can, using only digging and jumping, without being taught a technique. (b) Ask
  an outsider to make themselves unable to get out. PASS if they can, and if the game then ends the
  attempt as a death rather than leaving them alive and stuck.
- **EVIDENCE.** Built player.
- **FORCES.** Jump height against mountable step height (**K5**, «насколько крутые уклоны и
  перепады»); whether the dig verb can cut the surface under the body; whether the body may dig
  while airborne. The value of K5 is NOT decided here — the row tests that the knob exists and that
  changing it changes how far a player can climb.

**ROW 4 — death restarts the level in full, and you are playing again at once.** (line 4 · criterion 9) `SETTING K2`

**REWRITTEN 2026-07-28 on his answer.** «Давай делать, если смерть, то возвращается рестарт

## §SEAM — where the excluded things touch the modelled thing

Criterion 13 excludes by name. What it does not say is what happens at the boundary, which is the
thing that forces an unbudgeted decision mid-build. Four seams exist and all four are decidable now.

1. **A held object inside the substance.** Picking an object up is permitted; substance-versus-loose-object
   physics is cut from the simulation. **Seam: nothing happens.** The object is unaffected and does
   not affect the substance. Checkable: carry an object into a pocket; neither changes.
2. **Two kinds meeting in the same space.** Reactions are excluded. **Seam: they mix and nothing
   else.** Checkable: join two pockets and confirm no event of any kind fires. This also removes the
   entire firing-threshold apparatus of the previous converge's §8 from the first core.
3. **A shaft too steep to leave.** Ready ladders and lifts are excluded. **Seam: this is the
   legitimate death of criterion 3**, not a soft-lock — row 3 test (b).
4. **Liquid.** Excluded from the first core, kept as headroom, and possibly a REPLACEMENT for gas
   rather than an addition. **Seam: none in this outcome, and its price is owed the day it is
   admitted** (`i-substance-states-unpriced`, which stays open for exactly that reason).
