### C. The substance

**ROW 9 — the breach is an event, and its concrete form is a setting.** (line 9 · criterion 6) `BUILD` `SETTING K1`

- **CHECK.** EYE, and it is the row the cut changes most: «чем шире рез, тем сильнее и быстрее
  пошло» is a claim about what a person sees, and no assertion replaces the seeing.

- **FORCES.** The event's inputs (aperture area, pocket contents, time since the cut); whether it
  has a fixed duration or ends on a condition; whether it is one event per breach or repeats as the
  aperture widens.
- **COUPLING, and it survives the cut because it is a property of the design and not of a test.**

  of whatever the first pocket holds)**, against the severity of the breach event (K1). So «первое
  вскрытие не убивает мгновенно» is a property of a **SET of four** — K1, capacity, base rate, K3 —

  chosen here. This agrees with row 15, which names capacity and the base's refill as the most
  load-bearing numbers in the rig.
- **THE TWO HALVES CAN COME APART, and the record says so first-hand.** `i-flow-model-unmeasured`

  changing the flow numerator moves the ENDPOINT and never the TIMESCALE. So a build where a wider

## §SEAM — where the excluded things touch the modelled thing

Criterion 13 excludes by name. What it does not say is what happens at the boundary, which is the
thing that forces an unbudgeted decision mid-build. **Four seams are of the SUBSTANCE and all four
are decidable now. A fifth exists, is not of the substance, and is NOT decidable here.**

1. **A held object inside the substance.** Picking an object up is permitted; substance-versus-loose-object
   physics is cut from the simulation. **Seam: nothing happens.** The object is unaffected and does
   not affect the substance.
2. **Two kinds meeting in the same space.** Reactions are excluded. **Seam: they mix and nothing
   else** — no event of any kind fires. This also removes the entire firing-threshold apparatus of
   the previous converge's §8 from the first core.
3. **A shaft too steep to leave.** Ready ladders and lifts are excluded. **Seam: this is the
   legitimate death of criterion 3**, not a soft-lock (row 3).
4. **Liquid.** Excluded from the first core, kept as headroom, and possibly a REPLACEMENT for gas
   rather than an addition. **Seam: none in this outcome, and its price is owed the day it is
   admitted** (`i-substance-states-unpriced`, which stays open for exactly that reason).
5. **THE RIG MEETS THE NETWORK — and unlike 1–4 it is NOT decidable here.** Row 5 puts the
   simulation's numbers — including **the step rate** — behind controls reachable from the built
   player, and row 16 requires two machines to agree about a world they both mutate. **The forced
   questions, born here rather than at task time:** who owns a knob's value in a two-machine session;
   whether the step rate must be identical on both machines and what a mismatch does; and whether
   each rig switch is per-PLAYER or per-SESSION — immortality for one player only is a different
   game from immortality for the session, and a level load by one player is either a session-wide
   reload or a split world. **ONE PROPERTY IS ALREADY FORCED:** a knob that changes the simulation
   must have exactly ONE value in force for a session, or row 16's «both dig in the same section» is
   false by construction. **The mechanisms and every magnitude are HOW → `PLAN` / step 4.** This seam
   is also where the machine budget lands: a step rate two machines must share is a cost constraint
   on both, not on his alone.
