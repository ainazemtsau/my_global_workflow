    question: "Is the air counter visible to the player, and how?"
    why: "Card criterion 6 makes air the player's counter; criterion 5 forbids an interface. They do not contradict, but they overlap, and it decides what a player can read on himself and on a teammate - without which handing air over is blind."
    options:
      - "Air is the lamp: the light radius shrinks as air runs down, and a hand-off makes a teammate visibly brighter. Good because it costs zero art, holds «без интерфейса» literally, is readable on yourself, on a teammate at distance and on a still, and makes the world darker as you run out. Bad because it couples light to air, so a design that later wants them separate pays to unpick it."
      - "A diegetic gauge on the canister, visible both on yourself and on a teammate. Bad because it is one more object. Good because a teammate's state stays readable."
      - "Nothing at all: breathing and gasping carry it. Bad because a stranger may not understand what killed him. Good because it is the most honest reading and it forces people to talk."
      - "A bar on screen. Bad because it breaks «без интерфейса» on every screenshot."
    recommendation: "Air is the lamp."
