  players go through a space filled with substance, one way, with no way back.
  The only thing they interact with is the substance: they detect it, pass
  through it, and move it. A run is a CHAIN of sections with rest stops between

  1. No cargo-transport mechanics of any kind. All player interaction is with the
     substance.
  2. The substance is an ENVIRONMENT, not a pursuer. It lies, accumulates, flows,
     is unevenly distributed and changes from player actions and its own sources.
     Nothing about it chases the player by script.
  3. One-way passage, no way back. Rooms have real height, and the vertical
     distribution of the substance is part of the problem, not decoration.
  4. Substance kinds are distinguishable at a glance — no instrument, no UI —
     verified on a person who did not take part in development.
  5. Air is the only counter; it drains differently in different substances and
     can be handed to a teammate.
